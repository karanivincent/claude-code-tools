# Cold read: the brief

A required step before the shots (`toon-video` step 6). "It reads as something else" came back in three rounds
running: hail as fire and rocks as snow (Gibeon r1 to r2), fear as cheering (Mona Lisa r2), then stones as posts
(Gibeon r3). Each time only the critic found it, after the render. A reader who knows the story sees what it meant to
draw; a reader who knows nothing sees what is drawn.

## What gets a read

Every new place, prop, effect (fire, smoke, hail, dust, a glow), material (stone, snow, water, cloth, metal) and face
(each expression the story uses). One still each, rendered from the real build at phone size (1080 wide or a 0.3
scale), framed as a shot would frame it. A thing that changes (a fire catching, a door opening) gets one still of
each state.

Stills carry no captions and no labels: render them clean. A sheet tool that burns the spoken word or a frame label
into each still tells the reader the story, and the read is worthless. Why: Gibeon r4's first batch was rendered with
the sheet's labels and had to be redone with clean frames.

## Who reads

A fresh, cheap agent (Sonnet or Haiku), one per batch of about ten stills, that has never seen the script, the plan
or the meaning sheet. Give it only the stills and the prompt below. Never name the story, the period or what a thing
is meant to be.

## The prompt

```text
You are looking at stills from a short cartoon. You know nothing else about it.
For each picture, answer in three short lines:
1. What is this thing (or place)? Name it in a few plain words. If you are not sure, say what else it could be.
2. What is happening in the picture?
3. If there is a face: what does the person feel? One word, then why you think so.
Do not guess a story. Say only what you see.
```

## Reading the answers

- A thing is read right when the agent's first name for it is the intended one, or a near synonym (stones: "rocks",
  "boulders"; not "posts", "stumps", "pillars").
- A face is read right when the feeling is the intended one or next to it (fear: "scared", "worried"; not
  "excited").
- Anything misread is redrawn (shape, colour, size, material, a detail that says what it is) and read again by a new
  fresh agent, until it reads. Note each miss and its fix in the story's fix notes.
- A still the agent says it cannot tell counts as a miss.

Cost: a few cents and a few minutes a batch, against a render, a critique and a fix round.
