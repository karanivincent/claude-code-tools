# Epiclings: the channel's thumbnail rules

"Great stories, told small": toon retellings of heists, escapes, cons and last stands, in Shorts. Project
`/Users/vince/Projects/epiclings`; its thumbnail notes and Results go in `docs/thumbnails/README.md` (start it on the
first run).

## Frames (toon)

- Stills from the story's film composition with the toon-world film template's `tools/render.mjs --stills <dir>
  <times> [scale]` (1080x1920). A thumbnail-only staging goes in the story's place files as an extra shot name.
- The moments menu from the critic's per-second table (`docs/stories/<slug>/review-*-scores.md`): its 4s and 5s,
  and the beat board.

## Rules only this channel has

- Titles, descriptions, tags and cards never name a modern source show, film or book, or its characters or makers
  (CLAUDE.md, sourcing rule). History is told from the record: a card's facts must be true.
- The bean-head faces carry one strong feeling well (anger, shock, a grin); use the big face close-ups.
- YouTube Studio: Claude in Chrome, the "Udongo" Chrome; it holds two channels (Bible in Clay and Epiclings): check
  Epiclings is active before anything. Nothing is posted without Vincent's yes for that video.
